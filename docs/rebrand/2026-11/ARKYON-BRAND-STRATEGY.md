# ARKYON-BRAND-STRATEGY — Phase 2

Status labels per §1.3. Public name: **`ARKYON`** (owner instruction, F-01). No suffix forms.

## 1. Brand essence and category

- **Essence:** the keystone — the engineered piece that makes an unstable assembly hold. ARKYON is the engineering partner that makes AI systems hold under production load, governance and audit.
- **Category:** applied AI R&D and intelligent-systems engineering (not a tools vendor, chatbot studio, API reseller, or generic consultancy).
- **Positioning (one sentence):** *ARKYON researches, engineers and operates intelligent systems that take practical business problems to production — with governance built in from the first architecture.* [PROPOSED — derived from owner brief §0.2 and the site's existing verified copy; no new factual claims]
- **Elevator description:** ARKYON designs, builds and runs AI agents, automation and the surrounding platform — from problem discovery and applied research through deployment and day-2 operations. When AI is the wrong tool, we say so. [PROPOSED]

## 2. Audience and buyer problem (per brief §0.3)

Primary: founders/CEOs with costly operational problems; CTOs/CIOs/architects; COOs and process owners. Buyer problem: AI pilots that never survive contact with production; unclear governance; integration risk. Site must answer: what do you build, how do you build it, what does it cost me to start, what happens after launch.

## 5. Personality, voice, copy rules

Carry the entire existing voice system forward (BRAND-GUIDE §2–4): precise, calm, operator-grade, honest, concrete, sparse. No exclamation marks. Forbidden-word list unchanged and still grep-enforced. Mandatory disclosure discipline unchanged (`src/data/disclaimers.ts` remains the only home of disclosure copy).

**Voice for ARKYON:** speaks like the engineer who has to keep the system alive at 3 a.m. — measures twice, labels everything, never sells certainty.

## 6. Messaging hierarchy

1. **Proposition:** intelligent systems, engineered to production.
2. **Supporting:** agents + automation + platforms + the infrastructure and governance around them.
3. **Proof:** architecture diagrams, method pages, labelled example architectures, working tools (readiness, ROI), editorial depth.
4. **CTA:** Start a Project (single header CTA invariant); secondary: Assess My AI Opportunity.

## 7. Terminology rules

- Public name exactly `ARKYON` in lockups, titles, metadata, RSS, JSON-LD, manifest — and in sentences: the derivation deliberately never title-cases the head token (an acronym brand must not become "Arkyon"), so a single-word brand renders as `ARKYON` everywhere, prose included.
- Prohibited: "ARKYON SYSTEMS/AI/LABS"; any legal-entity implication; "guaranteed", "certified" (without truth), forbidden-word list.
- Historical word "VANTIQ" permitted **only** in dated records (`docs/rebrand/2026-11/`, git history) and regression fixtures.
